SSC Maths Questions

  1. A train travels 360 km at a uniform speed. If the speed had been 5 km/h more, it would have taken 1 hour less. Find the original speed of the train.
  2. The ratio of ages of A and B is 4 : 5. After 8 years, the ratio becomes 5 : 6. Find their present ages.
  3. A shopkeeper marks an article 25% above the cost price and allows a discount of 10%. Find his profit percentage.
  4. A sum becomes ₹14,400 in 2 years at compound interest compounded annually at 20%. Find the principal.
  5. Two pipes can fill a tank in 12 hours and 18 hours respectively. In how many hours will they fill the tank together?
  6. Find the least number which when divided by 12, 18, and 30 leaves remainder 5 in each case.
  7. A man spends 75% of his income. His income increases by 20% and expenditure by 10%. Find the percentage increase in savings.
  8. A boat can travel 30 km downstream in 2 hours and the same distance upstream in 3 hours. Find the speed of the stream.
  9. A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 7 : 3. If 20 litres of water is added, the ratio becomes 7 : 5. Find the original quantity of mixture.
  10. Simplify: 48 ÷ 6 × 4 + 18 − 12.
  11. The average of 15 numbers is 32. If one number is excluded, the average becomes 30. Find the excluded number.
  12. A man can do a work in 24 days and another man in 36 days. In how many days will they complete the work together?
  13. Find the simple interest on ₹8,000 at 7.5% per annum for 3 years.
  14. The population of a town increases by 10% annually. If the present population is 48,400, find the population after 2 years.
  15. The difference between compound interest and simple interest on ₹5,000 for 2 years at 10% per annum is?
  16. Find the HCF and LCM of 72 and 120.
  17. A number exceeds its two-fifth by 36. Find the number.
  18. A train 180 metres long crosses a platform 220 metres long in 20 seconds. Find the speed of the train.
  19. If the selling price of 12 articles equals the cost price of 15 articles, find the profit percentage.
  20. Divide ₹7,200 among A, B, and C in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4.
  21. A cyclist covers 15 km in the first hour, 20 km in the second hour, and 25 km in the third hour. Find the average speed.
  22. A person bought an item for ₹2,400 and sold it at a loss of 15%. Find the selling price.
  23. Find the value of √2025 + √1225.
  24. A tank is 40% full. If 120 litres are added, it becomes 70% full. Find the capacity of the tank.
  25. The ratio of the present ages of a father and son is 7 : 2. After 10 years, the ratio becomes 9 : 4. Find the father's present age.
  26. A trader mixes rice costing ₹40/kg with rice costing ₹60/kg in the ratio 3 : 2. Find the average cost price per kg.
  27. Find the compound interest on ₹12,000 for 2 years at 8% per annum.
  28. A car travels at 72 km/h. Find the distance covered in 25 seconds.
  29. The perimeter of a rectangle is 84 cm and its length is 26 cm. Find its breadth.
  30. A sum of money doubles itself in 8 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become four times?
  31. A can complete a work in 10 days and B in 15 days. They work together for 3 days. What fraction of work is left?
  32. Find the next number in the series: 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ?
  33. A shopkeeper gains 20% after giving a discount of 10% on the marked price. Find the marked price if the cost price is ₹1,500.
  34. A train moving at 54 km/h crosses a pole in 12 seconds. Find the length of the train.
  35. A man invested ₹12,000 at 12% simple interest. Find the amount after 4 years.
  36. Find the square root of 576.
  37. A can do a work in 15 days and B in 20 days. They started together but A left after 5 days. In how many more days will B finish the remaining work?
  38. A student scores 72, 65, 80, 76, and 87 in five subjects. Find the average marks.
  39. Find the ratio of 2 hours to 45 minutes.
  40. A train leaves station A at 8:00 AM at 60 km/h. Another train leaves station B towards A at 9:00 AM at 80 km/h. The distance between A and B is 420 km. At what time will they meet?
  41. A number is increased by 25% and then decreased by 20%. Find the net percentage change.
  42. The product of two numbers is 864 and their HCF is 12. Find their LCM.
  43. Find the area of a circle whose radius is 14 cm.
  44. If 18 men can complete a work in 20 days, how many men are needed to complete it in 12 days?
  45. The ratio of milk to water in a mixture is 5 : 1. If 12 litres of water is added, the ratio becomes 5 : 2. Find the quantity of milk.
  46. A sum amounts to ₹16,200 in 3 years at simple interest at 8% per annum. Find the principal.
  47. Simplify: (25 × 16) ÷ (5 × 4).
  48. A person travels one-third of a journey at 30 km/h, one-third at 45 km/h, and the remaining at 60 km/h. Find the average speed for the whole journey.
  49. The difference between two numbers is 48 and their ratio is 5 : 3. Find the numbers.
  50. A dealer offers two successive discounts of 20% and 10% on an article marked ₹5,000. Find the final selling price.
